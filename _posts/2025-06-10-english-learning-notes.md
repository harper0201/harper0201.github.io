### Deal
- **Meaning**: An agreement or arrangement, especially in business or politics.
- **Example**: They signed a new trade **deal** last week.

### Scheme
- **Meaning**: A plan, especially a secret or dishonest one.
- **Example**: He was involved in a tax evasion **scheme**.

### Giving the Go-Ahead
- **Meaning**: Granting permission to begin something.
- **Example**: The manager **gave the go-ahead** for the project to start.

### Slash
- **Meaning**: To reduce drastically.
- **Example**: The company plans to **slash** prices by 50% during the sale.

### Kick Off
- **Meaning**: To begin or start something.
- **Example**: The campaign **kicked off** with a big event downtown.

### Close the Loop
- **Meaning**: To finalize or complete a communication or task cycle; to follow up and ensure everything is resolved.
- **Example**: I just wanted to **close the loop** on our earlier conversation about the software update.

### White-Collar / Blue-Collar
- **White-Collar**: Related to office or professional work.  
- **Blue-Collar**: Related to manual labor or industrial work.  
- **Example**: The factory employs mostly **blue-collar** workers, while the headquarters is full of **white-collar** employees.

## 🔹 Communication & Conflict

### Tit for Tat
- **Meaning**: A retaliation or response in kind.
- **Example**: The two countries are stuck in a **tit-for-tat** trade war.

### Downplay
- **Meaning**: To make something seem less important than it really is.
- **Example**: The company tried to **downplay** the financial losses.

### Use Warning
- **Meaning**: To express caution or alert before an action or decision.
- **Example**: The teacher **used a warning** tone when discussing deadlines.

---

## 🔹 Investigation & Analysis

### Piece Together
- **Meaning**: To reconstruct or understand something by putting parts or clues together.
- **Example**: Detectives are **piecing together** what happened at the crime scene.

### Motive
- **Meaning**: A reason for doing something, especially something hidden or dishonest.
- **Example**: The police are still searching for a **motive** behind the attack.

---

## 🔹 General Use

### Ditch
- **Meaning**: To abandon or get rid of something.
- **Example**: She **ditched** her old phone for a new one.

### On the Verge Of
- **Meaning**: About to do or experience something.
- **Example**: He was **on the verge of** quitting his job.

### Likewise
- **Meaning**: In the same way or similarly.
- **Example**: She enjoyed the concert, and I felt **likewise**.

### Footage
- **Meaning**: Recorded video material.
- **Example**: The security camera **footage** revealed the suspect's identity.

### Plague
- **Meaning**: (Literal) A contagious disease; (Figurative) To trouble or bother persistently.
- **Example**: Financial problems have **plagued** the business for years.
