### Shot in the dark
- **Meaning:** A guess or attempt made with very little knowledge or certainty.
- **Example:** The fix I tried was a shot in the dark, but surprisingly, it worked.
- **Origin:** Comes from the idea of firing a gun in complete darkness, where you can't see your target—so hitting anything would be pure luck.

### On the map
- **Meaning:** To become well-known or famous—usually for a place, person, or thing that wasn’t previously known.
- **Example:** Her performance in that movie put her on the map as an actress.

### Live and learn
- **Meaning:** Used to express acceptance of a mistake or failure, with the idea that life teaches you lessons.
- **Example:** Tried fixing my laptop and made it worse. Live and learn, I guess.

### True that
- **Meaning:** “I agree” or “That’s right.”
- **Example:** “Hard work pays off in the end.” “True that!”

### Middle of nowhere
- **Meaning:** A very remote or isolated place, far from cities or conveniences.
- **Example:** We broke down in the middle of nowhere — no cell signal, no gas station, nothing.

### Tourist trap
- **Meaning:** A place that attracts tourists but is overpriced or low quality.
- **Example:** Gotta love a tourist trap.

### Wonky
- **Meaning:** Not working properly, unstable, or noticeably off.
- **Example:** My Wi-Fi’s been acting wonky all morning.

### Money doesn’t grow on trees around here
- **Meaning:** We don’t have unlimited money; we have to be careful with spending.
- **Example:** Let’s skip the fancy restaurant—money doesn’t grow on trees around here.

### Take the floor
- **Meaning:** To begin speaking in a formal or public setting.
- **Example:** Please take the floor and begin your presentation.

### I have no clue
- **Meaning:** I don’t know or have no idea.
- **Example:** Do you know why the system crashed? No clue.

### Ouch, that’s rough
- **Meaning:** That sounds painful, difficult, or unfortunate.
- **Example:** “I studied all night and still failed the test.” "Ouch, that’s rough."

### Got your hands full
- **Meaning:** You’re very busy or dealing with a lot.
- **Example:** “Can you help with this?” “Wish I could, but I’ve got my hands full with the new project.”

### Pop in
- **Meaning:** To visit or enter quickly or briefly.
- **Example:** I just wanted to pop in and say hi.

### Chicago, huh?
- **Comment:** Windy city—never been. Always wanted to check it out, though.

### It’s overrated
- **Meaning:** Not as good as people say.

### They eat that stuff up
- **Meaning:** They really enjoy it or are totally into it.
- **Example:** He tells those dramatic stories at every meeting, and they eat that stuff up.

### Gotta pay the bills somehow
- **Meaning:** Doing a job you don’t love, just to make money.
- **Example:** Acting isn't my passion, but hey, gotta pay the bills somehow.

### Put me down
- **Meaning:** Register or sign someone up.
- **Example:** Can you put me down for the 3 PM meeting?

### Been in a bit of a slump
- **Meaning:** Things haven’t been going well lately.
- **Example:** I’ve been in a bit of a slump recently.

### Fair enough
- **Meaning:** I can accept that.
- **Example:** “I can’t help today, I’m swamped.” “Fair enough.”

### Lean to
- **Meaning:** Tend toward or prefer something.
- **Example:** I usually lean to the practical side when making decisions.
